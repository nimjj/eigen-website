## Real Automation, Not Just Connected Apps

Most automation tools handle one hand-off at a time: a form fills, a row gets added, a message goes out. n8n is built for more than that. Every workflow is visual, fully open, and extendable with real code, so teams use it to automate entire processes end to end — the kind of work that today still runs on five tools, three people, and a shared spreadsheet holding it all together.

Done properly, that's not a small productivity tweak. It's hours of manual coordination quietly disappearing from your team's week, every week.

## Five Workflows That Take Real Work Off Your Plate

Not single-step triggers — full processes, wired start to finish.

### The Order-to-Cash Engine
An order lands from your store, marketplace, or sales form. n8n checks live stock, generates and sends the invoice, kicks off fulfillment with your carrier or 3PL, updates the accounting system, and syncs the customer record in your CRM. The only thing that ever reaches a human is an exception — a backorder, a failed payment, a bad address.

### Lead-to-Revenue, Fully Wired
A lead fills a form or books a call. n8n enriches it, scores it against your ideal customer profile, creates or updates the CRM record, assigns the right rep, fires off a Slack alert, and drops the lead into the right follow-up sequence, while a live dashboard tracks every stage it moves through. Sales spends its time selling, not logging.

### Invoice & AP, On Autopilot
An invoice lands by email. n8n extracts the line items, matches them against the purchase order and the goods receipt, routes anything above threshold for approval, and posts everything approved straight to your accounting platform, with a full audit trail behind it. What used to take days of chasing paper now takes minutes.

### Support Tickets That Triage Themselves
A ticket comes in. n8n classifies it by topic and urgency, checks it against your help center for an instant answer, routes whatever's left to the right queue, tracks it against SLA, and fires a CSAT survey the moment it's resolved, with a weekly performance rollup landing in your inbox on its own.

### Employee Onboarding, Day-One Ready
HR marks someone as hired. n8n provisions their accounts, sends onboarding paperwork out for e-signature, books their first-week meetings, enrolls them in training, and opens an IT ticket for their equipment, so day one feels ready instead of improvised.

## Put an AI Agent in the Driver's Seat

Every workflow above can run on fixed logic. Add an AI agent — n8n's agent nodes run on LangChain under the hood — and the workflow stops just executing steps. It starts making calls: reading the request, choosing which tool applies, checking its own confidence, and knowing when to act versus when to hand off to a person. Every decision and tool call is logged along the way, so what you get is full visibility, not a black box.

A few examples of what that looks like in practice:

**The support agent that actually resolves things.** Instead of just routing a ticket, it reads the full thread, searches your knowledge base and past resolutions, drafts a reply, and either sends it — when it's confident — or escalates with its reasoning attached.

**The finance agent that knows what it doesn't know.** Clean, clearly-matched invoices get approved and posted automatically. Anything unusual — a new vendor, a mismatched total, a possible duplicate — gets flagged to a human, with the specific reason why attached.

**The ops agent that watches the whole picture.** One agent monitors orders, support volume, and inventory together, and decides in real time whether to reorder stock, alert a manager, or pause a promotion, instead of three static workflows that never talk to each other.

### What an Agent Like This Can Plug Into
It isn't limited to a handful of tools — it can reach into practically anything your business already runs:

- **CRM & Sales** — HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive
- **Support & Ticketing** — Zendesk, Intercom, Freshdesk
- **Finance & Accounting** — QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite, Stripe
- **Communication** — Slack, Microsoft Teams, Gmail, WhatsApp/Twilio
- **Data & Storage** — PostgreSQL, Google Sheets, Airtable, BigQuery
- **Knowledge Bases** — Pinecone, Qdrant, Notion, Confluence
- **Infrastructure** — AWS, Google Cloud, Azure
- **And beyond** — any internal API, legacy system, or custom tool reachable by request

The result isn't just automation anymore. It's a process that runs itself, watches itself, and only pulls a person in when it genuinely matters.
